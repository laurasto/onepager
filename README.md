# OnePager

Welcome to my onepager! I am Exploring ways to make our standard OnePagers more personal as well as user friendly here.

## Why OnePager Matters

Onepager is useful for several reasons:

- **Accessibility**: It provides easy access to information for all stakeholders.
- **Consistency**: It ensures consistency in communication, reducing confusion and errors.
- **Onboarding**: New team members can quickly familiarize themselves with our people and their special interests/skills.
